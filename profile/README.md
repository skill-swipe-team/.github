# 👋 Welcome to SkillSwipe!

### 🚀 Swipe. Match. Build.

**SkillSwipe** is a platform that connects startup founders with talented specialists who want to bring exciting ideas to life.

Finding the right people for your startup shouldn't be complicated. SkillSwipe makes team building simple, engaging, and intuitive through a swipe-based matching experience.

### 💡 How it works

- 👤 **Create a profile** — Share your skills, experience, or startup idea.
- 🔍 **Discover opportunities** — Explore relevant experts and startup projects.
- 💜 **Swipe & Match** — Express your interest and connect when it's mutual.
- 💬 **Start collaborating** — Chat with your matches and build something amazing together.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Frontend:** Telegram Mini App
- **Database:** PostgreSQL, pgvector
- **Telegram Integration:** aiogram 3
- **AI & Matching:** LLM, embeddings, recommendation algorithms

### 👩‍💻 About Our Team

We are a team of four developers building SkillSwipe as a collaborative software engineering project.

Our goal is to make it easier for innovative ideas to find the right people — and for talented people to discover projects worth building.
